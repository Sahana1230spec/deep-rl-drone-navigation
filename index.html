Deep Reinforcement Learning for Drone Navigation in Simulated 3D Environments

Autonomous UAV navigation using PPO, SAC, and TD3 trained in Microsoft AirSim. This project demonstrates end-to-end learning of collision-free flight policies from raw sensor observations across multiple simulated environments and UAV dynamics models.

Simulation Environments

Three distinct AirSim environments for training and evaluation

SimpleAvoid Environment
Baseline training environment with sparse, symmetric obstacles. Includes 10 static obstacles arranged in a grid pattern. Ideal for initial convergence verification and stable policy learning.
Random Obstacles
Procedurally generated environment with varied obstacle shapes (cylinders, cones, cubes). Helps evaluate generalization and robustness under dynamic variation.
Urban City Environment
High-rise buildings, narrow corridors, shadows, and complex urban geometry. Ideal for long-horizon planning and partial observability challenges.

Deep RL Algorithm Comparison

Comprehensive analysis of PPO, SAC, and TD3 performance characteristics

PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization):
Stable learning with moderate sample efficiency. Smooth reward progression and consistent crash-rate reduction. Ideal for safety-critical applications.
SAC (Soft Actor-Critic):
High sample efficiency, faster convergence, strong exploration, near-perfect success rate for best architectures. Great for complex tasks.
TD3 (Twin Delayed DDPG):
Deterministic, stable, low-variance trajectories. Rapidly reaches high reward and consistent success rates. Excellent for reproducible control.

PPO

Stable on-policy method with smooth learning curves.

  • Highly predictable training
  • Great for safety-sensitive missions

SAC

Best for rapid prototyping and complex exploration-heavy environments.

  • Fastest convergence
  • Best generalization

TD3

Deterministic policy with superior consistency and reproducibility.

  • Low-variance control
  • Great for industrial UAV deployments

Conclusion & Future Work

This project demonstrates the viability of Deep Reinforcement Learning for autonomous UAV navigation. PPO, SAC, and TD3 all reached high performance across varied environments. Future work includes sim-to-real transfer, LiDAR/stereo-based navigation, domain randomization, and multi-agent coordination.
